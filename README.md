# Customer-Spending-Pattern-Analysis-Banking-Offers-Segmentation-
This project focuses on analyzing transaction data from 500+ banking customers to uncover spending patterns, identify high-value customers, and detect Drop-off-risk segments. The goal was to support data-driven marketing strategies and improve customer engagement through personalized offers.

**Problem Statement**
Banks generate millions of transaction records every day, but without proper analysis, this data often remains underutilized. As a result, banks face difficulties in identifying top spending categories, understanding customer payment preferences, designing personalized offers, and retaining high-value customers. The lack of data-driven insights limits opportunities for revenue growth and reduces customer loyalty.

**Project Objectives**

1.The objective of this project is to analyze customer transaction data to gain meaningful insights that support better banking decisions. Specifically, the project focuses on:

2.Analyzing customer spending patterns across categories such as Shopping, Travel, Food, and Bills

3.Identifying preferred payment modes including UPI, Cash, Credit Card, and Debit Card

4.Segmenting customers based on income levels and age groups

5.Providing actionable insights to design personalized banking offers

6.Improving customer satisfaction and optimizing revenue opportunities

**Dataset Overview**

The dataset contains 500 transaction records collected over a period from January to May 2024.
**It includes the following key fields:**

Customer ID , Age and Gender , Income Level , Transaction Category , Transaction Amount , Payment Mode , Transaction Date

**Key Dashboard Insights**

1.Top Spending Category: Bills (₹1.01M)

2.Total Transaction Value: ₹4.99M

3.Dominant Income Group: Medium Income (₹2.28M)

4.Most Used Payment Mode: Cash (₹1.38M)

5.Spending by Gender:

6.Male Customers: ₹2.6M

7.Female Customers: ₹2.4M

**Key Insights**

1.A majority of customer transactions are related to bill payments, making it the highest spending category.

2.Cash remains the most commonly used payment mode, contributing approximately ₹1.3M in transaction value.

3.Adult customers account for the highest transaction amounts compared to other age groups.

4.Customers in the medium-income group generate the largest share of total transactions.

5.Spending behavior between male and female customers is nearly balanced, with no significant gender-based difference.

**Recommendations**

1.Introduce a 2% cashback on UPI transactions to encourage customers to shift from cash to digital payments.

2.Offer lower interest rate credit cards to elderly and young customers, while increasing credit limits for adults to maximize usage.

3.Provide low-interest loan options for elderly customers to support financial stability and increase retention.

4.Offer 10% discounts on Shopping, Entertainment, and Travel when payments are made using debit cards to boost card usage.

5.Increase fixed deposit (FD) interest rates by 1% to attract more customers toward long-term savings.

6.Send targeted promotional messages to medium-income customers informing them about the latest offers and benefits.

7.Allow extended repayment periods on credit cards to improve customer convenience and reduce payment-related stress.
